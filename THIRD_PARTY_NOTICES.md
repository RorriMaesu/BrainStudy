# Third-party notices

## BigBrain Project cortical surface

BrainStudy includes modified versions of:

- `bigbrain_left_hemisphere.stl`
- `bigbrain_right_hemisphere.stl`

Source: BigBrain Project 3D print models, Forschungszentrum Jülich / McGill University.

License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). A copy of the source license is included at `public/models/BIGBRAIN-LICENSE.txt`.

Modifications made for BrainStudy:

- duplicate vertices merged;
- coordinate system centered and normalized for browser display;
- STL geometry converted to binary glTF (`.glb`);
- approximate lobe-level teaching colors assigned from gross spatial position.

Citation:

Amunts K, Lepage C, Borgeat L, et al. BigBrain: An ultrahigh-resolution 3D human brain model. Science. 2013;340(6139):1472–1475. doi:10.1126/science.1235381.

The lobe colors are educational overlays and do not represent atlas-grade cytoarchitectonic boundaries. Deep structures and pathways in BrainStudy are original schematic teaching reconstructions and are not derived from the BigBrain mesh.
